# Privacy

Solitaire collects nothing and sends nothing anywhere. It asks for no permissions, and has no
internet permission, so it cannot.

It keeps, in its own private storage on the phone: the game of each kind you have under way
(the deal and the moves made since), which game was open, the two settings, and how many games
of each kind you have played and won. None of it is backed up by the app, and uninstalling it
removes all of it.

The About dialog's last line opens a web address in your browser, and its "Write to
email@wanderthe.dev" row opens your email app, each only when you press it. The app talks to no
outside service.

The one permission in its manifest is not one you are asked for: Android's support library adds
it to every app built with it (`DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION`), so that the app's own
internal messages can reach only the app itself.

## Who we are

Solitaire is made by wander wildwood, an independent developer: one person, not a company. wander wildwood is the data controller for anything the app itself handles, and that is very little. Solitaire keeps what you put in it on your phone and sends nothing to the developer, so the developer holds no data about you.

## How to contact us

Write to email@wanderthe.dev about your data, this policy or anything else. The same address is in the app, under About.

## Your rights

If you are in the EU, the EEA or the UK, the GDPR gives you the right to:

- see the personal data held about you (access)
- have it corrected (rectification) or deleted (erasure)
- limit how it is used (restriction)
- object to its use (objection)
- get a copy to take elsewhere (portability)
- withdraw your consent at any time, where something rests on it
- complain to a data protection supervisory authority, for example the one where you live

Similar laws elsewhere, such as the CCPA in California, give you the right to know, delete and correct, and to opt out of the sale or sharing of your data. Nothing from Solitaire is sold or shared.

Since Solitaire keeps what it stores only on your phone, you can exercise most of these rights yourself: clear the app's data in Android's settings, or uninstall the app, which deletes all of it. For anything you cannot do yourself, write to the address above.
